Romanian Whist on the web

A mock-up of the browser client planned in the engine's docs/web-client-plan.md. It wears the desktop client's look, as the plan requires, and adds what the desktop client doesn't have: online tables, a lobby, players who drop and come back, and a turn timer. The cards, portraits and table are the desktop client's own art; the game states are sample data.

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger view.

The table space, seat places, hand fan, played-card positions and corner panels follow the desktop client's source (src/table/), and the colours and fonts its theme (src/paint/Theme.cpp). New pieces (the lobby, presence on the name panels, the countdowns, the notices) are drawn in the same style, as the plan's section 8.1 asks. The address whist.example stands in for the real host.
